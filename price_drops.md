## RAM (Memory)

### Market News
2026 is experiencing a major DDR5 memory crisis driven by AI demand. Data centers are consuming an estimated 20% of all DRAM production, and Samsung has more than doubled DDR5 contract prices (from ~$7 to ~$19.50 per unit). 32GB DDR5 kits that sold for $80–$120 in mid-2025 now list for $300–$500. The supply shortage is structural: manufacturers find building HBM for AI GPUs 10× more profitable per wafer than consumer DDR5. Winbond and SK Hynix have capacity booked solid through 2027. **Industry consensus: buy now if you need RAM — prices are expected to keep climbing into 2027.** Current Newegg promo codes (valid July 14–31) represent some of the best discounts available.

## Solid State Drives (SSDs)

### Market News
SSD prices have surged dramatically in 2026. NAND flash prices rose ~246% since early 2025, with consumer NVMe prices up ~115% overall. The crisis stems from AI data centers hoarding enterprise NAND — Western Digital's CEO stated they are "essentially sold out for all of 2026." A concurrent hard drive shortage accelerated data center migration to SSDs, further pressuring supply. Tariffs add an estimated 10–13% on top of demand-driven price increases. PCIe 5.0 drives now offer 12,000+ MB/s reads but provide minimal real-world gains for gaming or desktop use — PCIe 4.0 remains the practical sweet spot. The 2TB capacity tier is the current best value. A new **Samsung 990 SSD** (successor to 990 Pro) launched July 14, 2026, starting at $529.99 for 2TB — not yet discounted. Price analysts do not expect significant SSD price drops until 2027.
