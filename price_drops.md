## RAM (Memory)

### Technology News
- **"RAMmageddon" in full effect:** AI hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Meta, Amazon) are consuming 20%+ of all DRAM production in 2026. Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron have pivoted cleanroom capacity toward HBM (High Bandwidth Memory), which earns 3–5× more revenue per wafer than conventional DDR5.
- **Price explosion:** A standard 32GB DDR5 kit that cost ~$95 in mid-2025 is now $350–600. Samsung raised DDR5 contract prices by over 100% in 2026. DDR4 32GB kits that were $60–90 in late 2025 now cost $150–180+.
- **SK Hynix started mass production of HBM4** (36GB/chip, up to 2 TB/s bandwidth) for Nvidia's Rubin AI accelerators — 60% faster than HBM3e but prioritized for enterprise, not consumers.
- **Micron broke ground on a new $9.3B fab** (announced July 8, 2026), but production won't begin until Q3 2028. CEO Sanjay Mehrotra expects the shortage to persist through 2027.
- **Newegg's 4th of July sale** (still active) is currently offering the best DDR5 prices consumers have seen in months, with promo codes unlocking additional savings.

## Solid State Drives (SSDs)

### Technology News
- **NAND prices stabilizing but remain elevated:** After enterprise SSD contract prices surged 53–58% in Q1 2026 alone (driven by AI data center buildout), NAND contract pricing in Q3 2026 is now tracking flat to slightly down (0–5%). This is early relief but consumer prices haven't followed yet.
- **Samsung began mass production of PCIe Gen 6 PM1763 enterprise SSDs** (4TB, 8TB, 16TB capacities; up to 28,400 MB/s sequential read) — primarily for AI data centers, not consumer use.
- **WD CEO revealed the company is "sold out for calendar 2026"**, with 89% of Q2 revenue coming from its cloud/enterprise segment. This leaves limited consumer-grade supply.
- **SanDisk doubled the price of enterprise 3D NAND** in Q1 2026, with effects cascading into the consumer market throughout the year.
- **Prime Day / July sales may be the best SSD pricing** until Black Friday, per market analysts — stock on popular models like WD SN850X and Crucial T705 is already running low.
- **PCIe Gen 5 consumer SSDs** (Crucial T710, Samsung 990 Pro 2) are available but at a premium; most buyers are best served by Gen 4 at current prices.
