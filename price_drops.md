## Tech News Roundup

**The 2026 Memory Crisis:** AI data centers are consuming an estimated 70% of global memory output in 2026 — up from ~20–30% in 2022. Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron have redirected manufacturing capacity toward High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) for AI accelerators, causing both DRAM and NAND Flash prices to surge. DDR5 RAM averages ~$19.95/GB, up over 400% in 2026. DRAM contract prices are projected to rise another 13–18% in Q3 2026, and NAND Flash prices are expected to climb 10–15%.

**NVIDIA halts new gaming GPU releases:** For the first time in roughly 30 years, NVIDIA reportedly plans no new gaming GPU in 2026, having cut RTX 50 series production by 30–40% to redirect memory supply toward higher-margin AI products. AMD similarly raised GDDR memory kit prices charged to board partners by ~10% effective July 2026 — its second increase in six months.

**Silver lining:** Retailers are running aggressive sale events (Newegg's FantasTech/4th of July sales, Amazon Prime Day) to move existing inventory. This creates a window for deals even as overall prices trend upward.
