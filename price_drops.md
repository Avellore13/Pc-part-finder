## Tech News

- **GPU market tight:** Current-gen GPU prices remain above MSRP across the board. AMD raised GDDR6 memory costs to board partners effective October 1, 2026, pushing prices higher. The RTX 5090 sits around 208% over MSRP and the RX 9070 XT is ~28% over street. RTX 5060 Ti remains one of the few cards holding near its launch price.
- **RAM prices elevated:** DDR5 prices remain high (~$16.25/GB) due to ongoing NAND/DRAM supply constraints. Crucial has exited the consumer memory market but continues to sell existing stock with warranty support.
- **SSD prices still above 2025 levels:** NVMe SSD prices remain stubbornly high, with Gen5 drives especially expensive. October Prime Day (ended Oct 7) produced some deals, but prices have mostly reverted. Gen3/SATA drives offer the best per-GB value.
