## 📌 Notes & Market Context (July 2026)

- **RAM prices are surging in 2026** due to AI datacenter demand pulling supply from consumer channels. DDR5 32GB kits that cost $60–$90 in late 2025 now run $150–$180+. Deals are rare and short-lived — act quickly when you see promo codes.
- **GPU supply has improved** for the RTX 50-series and RX 9000-series, with prices slowly approaching MSRP amid Prime Day / 4th of July promotions.
- **SSD prices are rising** across the board driven by NAND/DRAM shortages. 2TB NVMe drives under $150 (common in 2024–2025) are largely gone. The deals above represent the best current value.
- **Microcenter** in-store pricing may undercut online retailers on select CPUs and bundled RAM; check local availability if you have a store nearby.
- Newegg promo codes expire frequently. If a listed code no longer works, check the product page for updated promotions.
- All prices are in USD and do not include tax or shipping unless noted.
