## 🧠 RAM (Memory)

### Latest News
- **DDR5 prices have surged ~485% year-over-year.** A basic 32GB DDR5-6000 kit now costs $375–$400, compared to under $70 in early 2025.
- **Root cause:** Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron have shifted production lines toward HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) for AI datacenter GPUs, which commands far higher margins. Every HBM chip produced means fewer consumer DDR5 sticks.
- **No meaningful relief expected until 2027**, per Gartner's "memflation" analysis. AMD projects DDR5 to remain elevated until at least 2028.
- **Bundle deals** remain the best way to save — pairing RAM with CPU or motherboard purchases at Newegg or Micro Center can unlock meaningful discounts.
- **Collateral damage:** Nintendo raised Switch 2 U.S. pricing by $50 (to $499.99) effective September 1, 2026, citing the DRAM shortage.
- **DDR6** is still years away from consumer availability; no standard or timeline has been finalized.

## 💾 SSDs (Solid State Drives)

### Latest News
- **SSD prices have roughly doubled since late 2025.** 1TB NVMe drives went from ~$45 to ~$90; 2TB drives that sold for $110–$130 in 2024 now range from $350–$480.
- **Root cause:** NAND flash manufacturers (Samsung, Micron, SK Hynix) are redirecting production to high-density enterprise and AI storage. The 1Tb NAND chip spot price rose from $4.80 to $10.70 since August 2025.
- **PCIe 5.0 Gen 2 is now mature** — second-gen controllers from Silicon Motion (SM2508) and Phison (E28) have eliminated the thermal throttling that plagued early Gen 5 drives. Modern PCIe 5.0 SSDs run at 6–7W under typical loads and no longer require active cooling.
- **New Gen 5 models available:** Crucial P510 (PCIe 5.0) and Samsung 9100 PRO (PCIe 5.0) are now widely available.
- **WD Black SN8100 4TB PCIe 5.0** available at $479.99 — the best high-capacity Gen 5 option.
- **Price recovery** not expected until late 2027 per Jefferies Equity Research, as new NAND fab capacity won't come online until then. Black Friday 2026 may offer temporary discounts.
