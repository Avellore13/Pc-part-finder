## Graphics Cards (GPUs)

### Latest News
- **RTX 5000 Super canceled:** NVIDIA has officially shelved plans for RTX 5000 Super refresh cards. Manufacturers have been informed that no Super variants are currently planned, primarily due to elevated memory costs making them unviable.
- **AMD RDNA 5 delayed:** AMD's next-generation RDNA 5 GPUs may not launch until 2028 according to board partners, leaving the RX 9000 series as AMD's top tier for the foreseeable future.
- **AMD Multi-Frame Generation:** AMD's MFG (Multi-Frame Generation) technology is now rolling out to RX 9000 series cards via driver update, bringing it closer to NVIDIA's frame generation performance advantage.
- **RTX 5090 price surge:** The RTX 5090 (launched at $1,999 MSRP) is projected to reach $5,000+ by end of 2026 due to AI workstation demand. NVIDIA is reportedly developing an ultra-flagship "RTX 5090 Ti" or TITAN-class card for late 2026.
- **Prime Day / 4th of July sales:** Newegg ran significant GPU discounts during July 4th week, bringing several RTX 50-series and RX 9000-series cards below MSRP.

## RAM (Memory)

### Latest News
- **Memory crisis deepens:** AI data centers consumed ~20% of total global DRAM production in 2026. Samsung and SK Hynix have warned the shortage may persist until 2027+. Consumer DDR5 prices rose up to 110% in Q1 2026 alone.
- **HBM diverting supply:** Major DRAM fabs (Samsung, Micron, SK Hynix) are shifting wafer capacity to HBM4 production for AI customers, reducing DDR5 supply for consumer PCs. Micron broke ground on a new $9.3B fab on July 8, 2026 — but production won't begin until Q3 2028.
- **Prices cooling slightly:** As of early July, price surges are beginning to moderate as consumers hit affordability limits, but AI demand is expected to keep DRAM prices elevated through Q3 2026.
- **Newegg 4th of July Sale:** Newegg ran notable DDR5 promotions during the July 4th holiday week from brands including Corsair, G.Skill, Kingston, and TeamGroup — some deals may still be active or recently expired.
- **Buy now advice:** Multiple analysts recommend purchasing DDR5 now before further price increases expected in Q4 2026.
