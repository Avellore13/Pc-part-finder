## RAM

**Market News:** DDR5 prices remain at historic highs in October 2026. A 32GB DDR5-6000 kit that cost ~$100 in late 2025 now runs $450+. Nanya Technology reports covering only 50–60% of customer demand and plans contract price hikes of up to 20%. DDR4 prices are also up ~24% over three months.
